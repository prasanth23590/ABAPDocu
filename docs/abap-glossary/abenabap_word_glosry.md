# ABAP word

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Word of the ABAP programming language. ABAP words are the [tokens](abentoken_glosry.md) of an [ABAP statement](abenabap_statement_glosry.md) and express its semantics. In ABAP statements, ABAP words are either used as [ABAP keywords](abenabap_keyword_glosry.md) or as additions. A single ABAP word can be both a keyword and an addition. Combinations of ABAP words form [ABAP language elements](abenabap_language_element_glosry.md) or [ABAP language element additions](abenabap_language_elmnt_add_glosry.md).
