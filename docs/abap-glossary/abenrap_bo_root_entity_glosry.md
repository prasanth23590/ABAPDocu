# RAP BO root entity

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [RAP BO entity](abenrap_bo_entity_glosry.md) that defines the behavior of a [CDS root entity](abenroot_entity_glosry.md). The RAP BO root entity is the top-most entity of the hierarchical structure of a [RAP business object](abenrap_bo_glosry.md) and it represents the RAP business object.
