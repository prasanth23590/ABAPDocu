# RAP total ETag

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A field of a [draft-enabled RAP BO](abendraft_rap_bo_glosry.md) that enables [optimistic concurrency checks](abenoptimistic_conc_control_glosry.md) during the transition form draft to [persistent data](abenrap_persistent_data_glosry.md).
