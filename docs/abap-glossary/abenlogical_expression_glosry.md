# logical expression

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md) [ABAP SQL](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abensql_glossary.md) [CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

Formulation of a condition for [operands](abenoperand_glosry.md). A logical expression is a single [relational expression](abenrelational_expression_glosry.md) or is made up of multiple relational expressions combined by [Boolean operators](abenboolean_operator_glosry.md). The result of a logical expression is a [truth value](abentruth_value_glosry.md), that is, either true or false.

- In general ABAP, logical expressions are used in [control statements](abencontrol_statement_glosry.md) or other statements that contain conditions, as well as in [conditional expressions](abenconditional_expression_glosry.md) and [Boolean functions](abenboole_function_glosry.md). For further details, see the topic [Logical Expressions (`log_exp`)](../abap-programming-language/abap-program-flow-logic/abenlogexp.md).

- In ABAP SQL, logical expressions are used in conditions, such as `WHERE` and `ON` conditions.

- In ABAP CDS, logical expressions are used in conditions, such as `WHERE` and `ON` conditions.
