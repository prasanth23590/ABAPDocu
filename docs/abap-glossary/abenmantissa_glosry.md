# mantissa

[General](abenabap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A mantissa is an uninterrupted sequence of a *+* sign or a *-* sign and a sequence of digits that can have a maximum of one period (*.*) as a decimal separator at an arbitrary position. A mantissa is used in [scientific notations](abenscientific_notation_glosry.md) for the representation of numbers.
