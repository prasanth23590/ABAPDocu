# ABAP user

[ABAP](../abap-programming-language/abap-overview/abentechno_glossary.md)

## Definition

In the context of an [AS ABAP](abenas_abap_glosry.md) also user for short.

1.  Defined in the AS ABAP by a [user name](abenuser_name_glosry.md) and a [user master record](abenuser_master_record_glosry.md).

2.  User (person or other system) that is logged on to the AS ABAP.

Once ABAP users have logged on to the AS ABAP and have been authenticated, they can access the functions and objects of the AS ABAP during their [user session](abenuser_session_glosry.md) in accordance with their [authorizations](abenauthorization_glosry.md).
