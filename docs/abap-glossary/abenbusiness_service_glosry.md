# RAP business service

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

RAP BS for short. A RAP business service is a [RESTful](abenrestful_glosry.md) [business service](abenbs_glosry.md) for the consumption of [RAP business objects](abenrap_bo_glosry.md). A RAP business service is published with an OData protocol.
