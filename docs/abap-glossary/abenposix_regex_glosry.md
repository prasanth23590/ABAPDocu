# POSIX regular expression

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md) [General](abenabap_glossary.md)

## Definition

[Regular expression](abenregular_expression_glosry.md) in accordance with [POSIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POSIX) standard 1003.2. ABAP supports [POSIX syntax](../abap-programming-language/abap-obsolete-language-elements/abenregex_posix_syntax.md) for regular expressions by implementing the [Boost.Regex Library](http://www.boost.org/doc/libs/1_31_0/libs/regex/doc/index.html) in the [ABAP kernel](abenkernel_glosry.md). Regular expressions in POSIX syntax are obsolete in ABAP and should be migrated to [PCRE regular expressions](abenpcre_regex_glosry.md), [XPath regular expressions](abenxpath_regex_glosry.md) or [XSD regular expressions](abenxsd_regex_glosry.md).
