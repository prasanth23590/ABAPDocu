# inheritance

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Generalization-specialization relationship between [classes](abenclass_glosry.md). Inheritance means that new [subclasses](abensubclass_glosry.md) are derived by adopting all [class components](abenclass_component_glosry.md) from [superclasses](abensuperclass_glosry.md). A subclass can also be made more specific by declaring new components and [redefining](abenredefinition_glosry.md) [methods](abenmethod_glosry.md).
