# DQL

[ABAP SQL](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abensql_glossary.md) [CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

Short for Data Query Language. Subset of [SQL](abensql_glosry.md). The statements in DQL perform read operations on [relational](abenrelational_database_glosry.md) [database tables](abendatabase_table_glosry.md). In the ABAP language, DQL is implemented predominantly by the statements for [read accesses](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abenabap_sql_reading.md) of [ABAP SQL](abenabap_sql_glosry.md). In [ABAP CDS](abenabap_cds_glosry.md), the DQL statement `SELECT` is embedded in [CDS DDL](abencds_ddl_glosry.md) for [CDS views](abencds_view_glosry.md). **Note:** Sometimes the statements of DQL are included in the term [DML](abendml_glosry.md).
