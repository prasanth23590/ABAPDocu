# UTF-8

[General](abenabap_glossary.md)

## Definition

8-bit [Unicode character representation](abenunicode_char_represent_glosry.md). [ASCII](abenascii_glosry.md) characters are represented by a single byte. Other European characters are represented by two bytes. Most Asian characters are represented by three bytes. The characters in the [surrogate area](abensurrogate_area_glosry.md) are addressed directly using four bytes.
