# substructure

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Structured [component](abenstructure_component_glosry.md) of a [nested structure](abennested_structure_glosry.md). The direct predecessor of a substructure is its [superstructure](abensuperstructure_glosry.md). A substructure can be declared as a [boxed component](abenboxed_component_glosry.md).
