# leaf entity

[CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md) [RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [CDS entity](abencds_entity_glosry.md) of a [CDS composition tree](abencds_composition_tree_glosry.md) of a [RAP business object](abenrap_bo_glosry.md) that does not have any [child entities](abenchild_entity_glosry.md). The [root entity](abenroot_entity_glosry.md) can also be a leaf entity.
