# precision

[General](abenabap_glossary.md)

## Definition

Number of significant [places](abenfract_digit_glosry.md) in a [decimal floating point number](abendecfloat_glosry.md). These are the places of the mantissa after the leading zeros. The precision is at least one and at most the length of the mantissa (16 or 34). See also [scaling](abenscale_glosry.md).
