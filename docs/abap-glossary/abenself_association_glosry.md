# self-association

[CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

An [association](abenassociation_glosry.md) in which the [association source](abenassociation_source_glosry.md) and the [association target](abenassociation_target_glosry.md) are the same. Also referred to as reflexive association.
