# identity transformation

[ABAP](../abap-programming-language/abap-overview/abentechno_glossary.md)

## Definition

[XSL transformation](abenxsl_transformation_glosry.md) called `ID` delivered by SAP for reading or writing the canonical formats [asXML](abenasxml_glosry.md) and [asJSON](abenasjson_glosry.md). Calls of this transformation are handled in special ways internally, depending on the formats involved.
