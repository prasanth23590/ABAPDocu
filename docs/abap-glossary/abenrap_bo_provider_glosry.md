# RAP BO provider

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A piece of software that provides the [transactional buffer](abentransactional_buffer_glosry.md) and the implementation of a [RAP BO](abenrap_bo_glosry.md) based on its [RAP behavior definition](abencds_behavior_definition_glosry.md).

- A [managed RAP BO](abenmanaged_rap_bo_glosry.md) is provided fully or partly by the [managed RAP BO provider](abenmanaged_rap_bo_prov_glosry.md).

- An [unmanaged RAP BO](abenunmanaged_rap_bo_glosry.md) is fully provided by the [unmanaged RAP BO provider](abenunmanaged_rap_bo_prov_glosry.md).

- The draft data of a [draft-enabled RAP business object](abendraft_rap_bo_glosry.md) is provided by the [managed](abenrap_man_draft_provider_glosry.md) or [unmanaged draft provider](abenrap_un_draft_provider_glosry.md).

- A [BOPF-based RAP BO](abenrap_bopf_bo_glosry.md) is provided by the [BOPF-managed RAP BO provider](abenbopf_man_rap_bo_prov_glosry.md).
