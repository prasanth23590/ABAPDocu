# method

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

[Procedure](abenprocedure_glosry.md) that is declared as a [component](abencomponent_glosry.md) of a [class](abenclass_glosry.md) or of an [interface](abenoo_intf_glosry.md). The two possible methods are [instance methods](abeninstance_method_glosry.md) and [static methods](abenstatic_method_glosry.md). A method can be implemented only in the [implementation part](abenimplementation_part_glosry.md) of its class, between [`METHOD`](../abap-programming-language/abap-program-structure/abapmethod.md) and [`ENDMETHOD`](../abap-programming-language/abap-program-structure/abapendmethod.md).
