# decimal place

[General](abenabap_glossary.md)

## Definition

Also referred to as a fractional digit. Decimal places contain the fractional part of a decimal number and are noted to the right of the decimal separator, regardless of the number system. In the case of [decimal numbers](abendecimal_number_glosry.md), the decimal places are the [places](abenplace_glosry.md) that come after the [decimal separator](abendecimal_point_separat_glosry.md).
