# PCRE regular expression

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

[Perl Compatible Regular expression](abenpcre_glosry.md) that is expressed in [PCRE syntax](../abap-programming-language/abap-processing-internal-data/abenregex_pcre_syntax.md) and processed by the [PCRE2 Library](https://www.pcre.org/) implemented in the [ABAP Kernel](abenkernel_glosry.md) or by the [PCRE1 Library](https://www.pcre.org/) of the [SAP HANA Database](abenhana_database_glosry.md). See also [XPath regular expression](abenxpath_regex_glosry.md), [XSD regular expression](abenxsd_regex_glosry.md) and [POSIX regular expression](abenposix_regex_glosry.md) (obsolete).
