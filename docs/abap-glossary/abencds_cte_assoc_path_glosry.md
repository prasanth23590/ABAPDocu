# CDS/CTE association path

[ABAP SQL](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abensql_glossary.md) [CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

A sequence of [CDS associations](abencds_association_glosry.md) and [CTE associations](abencte_association_glosry.md) that associate [CDS entities](abencds_entity_glosry.md) and [common table expressions](abencommon_table_expression_glosry.md) with [association targets](abenassociation_target_glosry.md). In other words, a mixture of CDS and CTE association paths. CDS or CTE association paths can be specified in the form of [SQL path expressions](abensql_path_expression_glosry.md) in [ABAP SQL](abenabap_sql_glosry.md).
