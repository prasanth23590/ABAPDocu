# BOPF-based RAP business object

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

BOPF-based RAP BO for short. A BOPF-based RAP BO is the result of the migration of a [CDS-based BOPF business object](abencds_bopf_bo_glosry.md) to a RAP business object. Special syntax statements for BOPF-based RAP BOs are `managed by BOPF` and `with BOPF draft`. The [transactional buffer](abentransactional_buffer_glosry.md) of a BOPF-based RAP BO is provided by the [BOPF-managed RAP BO provider](abenbopf_man_rap_bo_prov_glosry.md). BOPF-based RAP BOs that are [draft-enabled](abendraft_rap_bo_glosry.md) are handled by the [BOPF-managed RAP draft provider](abenbopf_man_rap_dra_prov_glosry.md).
