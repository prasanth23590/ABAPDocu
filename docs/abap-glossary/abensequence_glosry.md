# sequence

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Also referred to as a series. [Control structure](abencontrol_structure_glosry.md) consisting of a [statement block](abenstatement_block_glosry.md) that is not defined by keywords and that is executed only once without a condition.
