# DML

[ABAP SQL](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abensql_glossary.md) [CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

Short for Data Manipulation Language. Subset of [SQL](abensql_glosry.md). The statements in DML perform [CUD](abencud_glosry.md) operations on [relational](abenrelational_database_glosry.md) [database tables](abendatabase_table_glosry.md). In the ABAP language, DML is implemented predominantly by the statements for [write accesses](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abenabap_sql_writing.md) of [ABAP SQL](abenabap_sql_glosry.md). **Note:** Sometimes the term DML also includes the statements of [DQL](abendql_glosry.md).
