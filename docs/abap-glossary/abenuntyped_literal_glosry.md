# untyped literal

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md) [ABAP SQL](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abensql_glossary.md) [General](abenabap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [literal](abenliteral_glosry.md) whose [data type](abendata_type_glosry.md) is implicitly derived from its value or its delimiter. Both untyped [numeric literals](abennumeric_literal_glosry.md) and untyped [character literals](abencharacter_literal_glosry.md) exist. See also [typed literal](abentyped_literal_glosry.md).
