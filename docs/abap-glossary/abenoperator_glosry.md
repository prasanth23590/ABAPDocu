# operator

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md) [CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md) [General](abenabap_glossary.md)

## Definition

Component of an [ABAP statement](abenabap_statement_glosry.md) or of a [CDS data definition](abencds_data_definition_glosry.md) that, when combined with [operands](abenoperand_glosry.md), forms an [expression](abenexp_glosry.md). When the statement is executed, an operation is executed for two operands connected by one operator, and the result is processed further in the statement. There are [arithmetic](abenarithmetic_operator_glosry.md), [bit](abenbit_operator_glosry.md), [string](abenstring_operator_glosry.md), [Boolean](abenboolean_operator_glosry.md), [relational](abenrel_operator_glosry.md), and [assignment operators](abenassignment_operator_glosry.md). There are also operators that do not combine operands, but perform other operations in [operand positions](abenoperand_position_glosry.md). Operators of this type are the [dereferencing operator](abendereferencing_operat_glosry.md), [declarations operators](abendeclaration_operator_glosry.md), and [constructor operators](abenconstructor_operator_glosry.md). Finally, the [literal operator](abenassignment_operator_glosry.md) is used to combine two [literals](abenabap_literal_glosry.md).
