# business service

[ABAP](../abap-programming-language/abap-overview/abentechno_glossary.md)

## Definition

BS for short. A business service makes [repository objects](abenrepository_object_glosry.md) from [AS ABAP](abenas_abap_glosry.md) available for consumption outside of AS ABAP. A business service is based on a [CDS service definition](abencds_service_definition_glosry.md) and a [service binding](abenservice_binding_glosry.md). Different communication protocols are available, such as OData, the [Information Access protocol](abenina_protocol_glosry.md), and [SQL services](abenabap_sql_services_glosry.md). In the context of the [ABAP RESTful Application Programming Model](abenarap_glosry.md), a business service is a [RESTful](abenrestful_glosry.md) service which can be called by a consumer. See [RAP business service](abenbusiness_service_glosry.md).
