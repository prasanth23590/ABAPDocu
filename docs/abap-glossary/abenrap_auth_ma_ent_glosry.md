# RAP authorization master entity

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [RAP BO entity](abenrap_bo_entity_glosry.md) that has its own authorization implementation. The implementation is used when requesting access to the authorization master entity or to any of its [authorization dependent entities](abenrap_auth_dep_ent_glosry.md).
