# LUW

[ABAP](../abap-programming-language/abap-overview/abentechno_glossary.md)

## Definition

Short for Logical unit of work. Time between two consistent states of the [database](abendatabase_glosry.md). See also [SAP LUW](abensap_luw_glosry.md) and [database LUW](abendatabase_luw_glosry.md).
