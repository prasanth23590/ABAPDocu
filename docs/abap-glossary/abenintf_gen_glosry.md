# interface (general)

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Boundary between two [contexts](abenobj_context_glosry.md).

- [Procedures](abenprocedure_glosry.md) have a [parameter interface](abenparameter_interface_glosry.md).

- In [classes](abenclass_glosry.md), interfaces are defined by [class components](abenclass_component_glosry.md) of the different [visibility sections](abenvisibility_section_glosry.md).

- The [public](abenpublic_glosry.md) components of classes can also be defined in standalone [interfaces](abenoo_intf_glosry.md) of [ABAP Objects](abenabap_objects_glosry.md).

- The [database interface](abendatabase_interface_glosry.md) connects an [AS ABAP](abenas_abap_glosry.md) to its [database](abendatabase_glosry.md).
