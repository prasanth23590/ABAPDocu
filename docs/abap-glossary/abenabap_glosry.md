# ABAP

[ABAP](../abap-programming-language/abap-overview/abentechno_glossary.md) [ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Advanced Business Application Programming. ABAP in the actual sense is the name of the [ABAP language](abenabala_glosry.md), but in a broader sense it is also the term for the entire [ABAP technology](abenabap_techno_glosry.md) provided for and by an [ABAP system](abenabap_system_glosry.md).
