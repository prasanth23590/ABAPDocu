# final

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Term in [ABAP Objects](abenabap_objects_glosry.md). A final [class](abenclass_glosry.md) cannot have any [subclasses](abensubclass_glosry.md). A final [method](abenmethod_glosry.md) cannot be [redefined](abenredefinition_glosry.md).
