# RAP BO service projection

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A RAP BO projection for a [RAP business service](abenbusiness_service_glosry.md). A service projection consists of a [CDS transactional query](abencds_transactional_pv_glosry.md) and a [RAP projection behavior definition](abencds_proj_bdef_glosry.md).
