# foreign key RAP business object

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [RAP business object](abenrap_bo_glosry.md) in a cross BO scenario that holds the [primary keys](abenprimary_key_glosry.md) or the [`%pid`](../abap-rap-business-objects/abap-for-rap-business-objects/abapderived_types_pid.md) of the associated [non-foreign key BO](abenrap_non_foreignkey_bo_glosry.md).
