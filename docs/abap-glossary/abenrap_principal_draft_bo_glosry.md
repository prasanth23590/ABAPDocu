# RAP principal draft BO

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [draft-enabled RAP business object](abendraft_rap_bo_glosry.md) in a [cross-BO association](abencds_cross_bo_assoc_glosry.md) scenario that assigns the draft state of its [instances](abeninstance_glosry.md) to the associated instances of the dependent draft RAP business object. All associated instances are set to draft and activated. A principal draft RAP business object has the syntax `with dependent draft` on the cross-BO association in the projection [BDEF](abencds_behavior_definition_glosry.md).
