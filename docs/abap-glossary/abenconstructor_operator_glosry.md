# constructor operator

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

[Operator](abenoperator_glosry.md) of a [constructor expression](abenconstructor_expression_glosry.md). Possible constructor operators are the [instance operator](abeninstance_operator_glosry.md) [`NEW`](../abap-programming-language/abap-creating-objects-and-values/abenconstructor_expression_new.md), the [value operator](abenvalue_operator_glosry.md) [`VALUE`](../abap-programming-language/abap-creating-objects-and-values/abenconstructor_expression_value.md), the [reference operator](abenreference_operator_glosry.md) [`REF`](../abap-programming-language/abap-processing-internal-data/abenconstructor_expression_ref.md), the [lossless operator](abenlossless_operator_glosry.md) [`EXACT`](../abap-programming-language/abap-processing-internal-data/abenconstructor_expression_exact.md), the [conversion operator](abenconversion_operator_glosry.md) [`CONV`](../abap-programming-language/abap-processing-internal-data/abenconstructor_expression_conv.md), the [component operator](abencorresponding_operator_glosry.md) [`CORRESPONDING`](../abap-programming-language/abap-processing-internal-data/abenconstructor_expr_corresponding.md), the [casting operator](abencasting_operator_glosry.md) [`CAST`](../abap-programming-language/abap-processing-internal-data/abenconstructor_expression_cast.md), the [reduction operator](abenreduce_operator_glosry.md) [`REDUCE`](../abap-programming-language/abap-program-flow-logic/abenconstructor_expression_reduce.md), the [filter operator](abenfilter_operator_glosry.md) [`FILTER`](../abap-programming-language/abap-processing-internal-data/abenconstructor_expression_filter.md), and the [conditional operators](abenconditional_operator_glosry.md) [`COND`](../abap-programming-language/abap-program-flow-logic/abenconditional_expression_cond.md) and [`SWITCH`](../abap-programming-language/abap-program-flow-logic/abenconditional_expression_switch.md).
