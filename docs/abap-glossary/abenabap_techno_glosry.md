# ABAP technology

[ABAP](../abap-programming-language/abap-overview/abentechno_glossary.md)

## Definition

The ABAP technology or [ABAP](abenabap_glosry.md) for short comprises the entire technology provided for and by an [ABAP system](abenabap_system_glosry.md) for developing and executing ABAP-based applications. ABAP technology supports the programming paradigms [ABAP Cloud](abenabap_cloud_glosry.md) and [classic ABAP](abenclassic_abap_glosry.md).
