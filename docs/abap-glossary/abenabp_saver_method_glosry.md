# RAP saver method

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

Special method in a [RAP saver class](abenabp_saver_class_glosry.md) of an [ABAP behavior pool](abenbehavior_pool_glosry.md) that is called during the [RAP save sequence](abenrap_save_seq_glosry.md). The names of saver methods are predefined and cannot be changed.&#x00A0;
