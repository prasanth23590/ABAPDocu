# cross-BO association

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

An [association](abencds_association_glosry.md) that is not part of a [RAP business object's](abenrap_bo_glosry.md) [composition tree](abencds_composition_tree_glosry.md), but has an [association target](abenassociation_target_glosry.md) that is part of a foreign business object. The opposite of a cross-BO association is an [intra BO association](abencds_intra_bo_assoc_glosry.md).
