# enhancement spot

[ABAP](../abap-programming-language/abap-overview/abentechno_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [repository object](abenrepository_object_glosry.md) used for the administration of explicitly created [enhancement options](abenenhancement_point_glosry.md). Enhancement spots are components of a tree structure hierarchy, whose branches represent [composite](abencomp_enhancement_spot_glosry.md) enhancement spots and whose leaves are [simple enhancement spots](abensimple_enhancement_spot_glosry.md). Enhancement points that are available implicitly do not have enhancement spots.
