# RAP business object behavior

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

The transactional behavior of a [RAP business object](abenrap_bo_glosry.md) that is defined in a [RAP behavior definition](abencds_behavior_definition_glosry.md).

It includes [entity behavior characteristics](abencds_entity_properties_glosry.md), [RAP BO operations](abenrap_bo_operation_glosry.md), [validations](abenrap_validation_glosry.md), and [determinations](abenrap_determination_glosry.md) all of which are either provided by the [managed RAP BO provider](abenmanaged_rap_bo_prov_glosry.md) or implemented in the [ABAP behavior pool](abenbehavior_pool_glosry.md) ([unmanaged RAP BO provider](abenunmanaged_rap_bo_prov_glosry.md)).
