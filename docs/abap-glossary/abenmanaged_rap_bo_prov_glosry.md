# managed RAP BO provider

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A part of the [RAP BO provider](abenrap_bo_provider_glosry.md) that provides [managed transactional buffers](abenmanaged_trans_buff_glosry.md) and managed behavior of [managed RAP BOs](abenmanaged_rap_bo_glosry.md) based on their [RAP behavior definition](abencds_behavior_definition_glosry.md).
