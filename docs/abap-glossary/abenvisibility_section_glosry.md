# visibility section

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

The [declaration part](abendeclaration_part_glosry.md) of a [class](abenclass_glosry.md) can be divided into up to three visibility sections. These areas are [public](abenpublic_glosry.md), [protected](abenprotected_glosry.md), or [private](abenprivate_glosry.md). They define the visibility of the [components](abenclass_component_glosry.md) of the class and therefore the [interfaces](abenintf_gen_glosry.md) of the class towards all users allowed by the [package check](abenpackage_concept_glosry.md) concept.
