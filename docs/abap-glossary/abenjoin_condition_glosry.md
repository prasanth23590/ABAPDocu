# join condition

[ABAP SQL](../abap-programming-language/abap-database-access/abensql_glossary.md) [CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

A [SQL condition](abensql_cond_glosry.md) or a [CDS DDL condition](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abap-cds-data-definitions/abencds_conditional_expression_v2.md) specified after the addition `ON` of a [join expression](abenjoin_expression_glosry.md). Also called `ON` condition.
