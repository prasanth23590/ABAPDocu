# CDS projected entity

[CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

Used in the context of [CDS projection views](abencds_projection_view_glosry.md). The [CDS view entity](abencds_v2_view_glosry.md) that is used as the basis of a CDS projection view, and that is defined after the addition `AS PROJECTION ON` is called projected entity.
