# friend

[ABAP Language](../abap-programming-language/abenabala_glossary.md)

## Definition

Term in [ABAP Objects](abenabap_objects_glosry.md). The additions [`FRIENDS`](../abap-programming-language/abap-declarations/abapclass_options.md) and [`LOCAL FRIENDS`](../abap-programming-language/abap-declarations/abapclass_local_friends.md) of the statement [`CLASS`](../abap-programming-language/abap-declarations/abapclass_definition.md) define other [classes](abenclass_glosry.md) or [interfaces](abenoo_intf_glosry.md) as friends of the class and thus grant them access to all of their components, regardless of the [visibility section](abenvisibility_section_glosry.md) or the addition [`READ-ONLY`](../abap-programming-language/abap-declarations/abapdata_options.md). In addition, friends of a class can create [instances](abeninstance_glosry.md) of the class without restrictions.
