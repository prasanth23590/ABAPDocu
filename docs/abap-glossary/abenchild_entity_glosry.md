# CDS child entity

[CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

Any [CDS entity](abencds_entity_glosry.md) of a [CDS composition tree](abencds_composition_tree_glosry.md) other than the [root entity](abenroot_entity_glosry.md). A child entity is defined as the [composition target](abencomposition_target_glosry.md) of a [to-child association](abento_child_association_glosry.md) in its [parent entity](abenparent_entity_glosry.md) and has a [to-parent association](abento_parent_association_glosry.md) to its parent entity. A child entity has exactly one parent entity.
