# RAP consumer hint

[RAP](../abap-rap-business-objects/abenrap_glossary.md)

## Definition

A RAP BO field or behavior characteristic that is propagated to the [consumer](abenrap_bo_consumer_glosry.md) through the chosen protocol, derived from [RAP feature control](abenrap_feature_control_glosry.md) or [authorizations](abenrap_auth_control_glosry.md). RAP consumer hints are intended to optimize the usability of the [business service](abenbusiness_service_glosry.md). For example, RAP consumer hints can result in fields being marked as [*mandatory*](abenmandatory_rap_bo_field_glosry.md) on a UI with an asterisk or in fields being grayed out when they are set to [read-only](abenread_only_rap_bo_field_glosry.md).
