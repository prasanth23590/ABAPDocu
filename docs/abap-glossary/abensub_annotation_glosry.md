# sub-annotation

[CDS](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abencds_glossary.md)

## Definition

[CDS annotation](abencds_annotation_glosry.md) that is specified in curly brackets `{ }` in an [annotation definition](abencds_anno_definition_glosry.md) after another annotation. When specifying a sub-annotation in [annotation syntax](abencds_annotation_syntax_glosry.md), curly brackets [`{ }`](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abap-cds-annotations/abencds_annotations_syntax.md) or [structured names](../abap-core-data-services-abap-cds/abap-cds-annotations/abencds_annotations_syntax_name.md) separated by periods are also used.
